# INC-2230 Duplicate settlement file (19 March 2025)

Severity: SEV-3. Status: resolved.

## Summary
On 19 March 2025 the nightly settlement export contained duplicate entries.

## Root cause
Change CHG-1050 moved the nightly settlement run from 01:00 UTC to 02:30 UTC. At 02:30 UTC the fraud-checker rule refresh job also writes to the export, and the two jobs overlapped.

## Impact
- settlement-batch: duplicate entries in the settlement export.
- fraud-checker: the rule refresh job ran concurrently with settlement.

## Resolution
The duplicates were removed manually within 1 hour. Action item: schedule the rule refresh job at 03:15 UTC.
