# INC-2211 Ledger write backlog (6 March 2025)

Severity: SEV-3. Status: resolved.

## Summary
On 6 March 2025 the ledger-service accumulated a backlog of about 41,000 pending writes. The backlog was cleared at 23:50 UTC.

## Root cause
Change CHG-1043 lowered the ledger commit timeout from 30 seconds to 10 seconds. After the payment gateway incident INC-2207 the gateway retried many requests at once, and commits that took longer than 10 seconds timed out and were queued again.

## Impact
- ledger-service: write backlog peaking at about 41,000 pending writes.
- settlement-batch: the nightly settlement run of 7 March started late because the ledger was still draining.

## Resolution
The team followed runbook-ledger-backlog.txt: paused settlement-batch, temporarily raised the commit timeout and drained the queue.
