Postmortem: INC-2207 payment processing delay

Total duration: 3 hours 10 minutes (09:12 to 12:22 UTC on 5 March 2025).

Root cause

Change CHG-1041 reduced the payment-gateway database connection pool from 200 to 50, which exhausted connections during the morning peak.

Services affected

  • payment-gateway
  • ledger-service
  • notification-service

Action items

  • Alert on connection pool saturation.
  • Require a load test for every connection pool change.
