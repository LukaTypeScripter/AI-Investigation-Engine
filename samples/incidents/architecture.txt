Orion Pay architecture overview

Services
- payment-gateway: receives card payments and calls ledger-service and fraud-checker for every authorisation.
- ledger-service: records every transaction and serves balances to merchant-portal.
- fraud-checker: scores each authorisation using velocity rules.
- notification-service: consumes payment events from payment-gateway and sends receipt emails.
- settlement-batch: reads the ledger every night and writes the settlement export used by finance.
- merchant-portal: dashboard for merchants, reads data from ledger-service.

Dependencies
payment-gateway depends on ledger-service and fraud-checker.
notification-service depends on payment-gateway events.
settlement-batch depends on ledger-service.
merchant-portal depends on ledger-service.
