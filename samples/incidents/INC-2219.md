# INC-2219 Delayed receipt emails (7 March 2025)

Severity: SEV-3. Status: resolved.

## Summary
Customers received payment receipt emails up to 2 hours late between 5 and 7 March 2025.

## Root cause
Change CHG-1042 enabled batched receipt emails and raised the notification-service flush interval from 5 seconds to 60 seconds. Together with the event backlog after the payment gateway incident INC-2207 the batches grew large and delivery lagged.

## Impact
- notification-service: delivery lag of up to 2 hours.

## Resolution
The flush interval was reduced to 10 seconds on 7 March 2025.
